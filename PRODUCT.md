# Product

## Business vision

Describes where you're going.

To be publishers' trusted partner in managing the content production process.

## Mission

Describes what you do today to get there.

Help publishers manage the content production process with greater clarity, control, and collaboration through Publisher Central—a product they can rely on.

## Goal

What you are trying to accomplish.

Deliver visibility, predictability, reliability, and a consistent cross-production experience.

---

## Product positioning

Make Publisher Central the primary interface where publishers experience TNQ's service and product offering as a brand—**Service as a Product (SaaP)**.

### SaaP

Transition from **"we do this for you"** → **"you can do this through our product"**.

---

## Key characteristics

- Users don't need intermediaries
- Standardized workflows
- Repetition
- Easily scalable with less cost
- System driven

## Product scope — key themes

1. **Initiating onboarding**
2. **Handling day-to-day operations**

## North star metric

**Increase the frequency of purposeful visits.**

### When do users return?

- If users can quickly see value → they return more often
- If users feel confident → they rely on the system regularly
- If users can do the desired action → visits become purposeful

A **purposeful visit** means the user leaves the product with **progress**, not just information.

---

## What makes a visit purposeful?

### Navigation signals — acted with intent

- Navigated to a specific article detail page
- Navigated to the Reports / Insights section
- Used filters or search to narrow down articles

### Interaction signals — acted, not just looked

Took an action or clicked on a page—for example:

- Raised an escalation
- Changed priority
- Uploaded a document
- Responded to a query
- Approved a correction
- Downloaded

### Behavioural signals

- Session duration above a minimum threshold (e.g. more than 90 seconds), ruling out accidental opens
- Visited more than one section in a single session

### Recurrence signals

The same user visits within a predictable window—for example, every Monday morning.

### Notification-triggered visit

They arrived via a platform notification or alert and then engaged beyond the landing point. This means the product successfully pulled them in with relevant context.
